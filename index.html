How to Use on Other Devices

Android phone, iPhone, Tablet, or another PC

Method 1: Local Wi-Fi Network (Instant Mobile Access)

If your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi router:

  1. Run a local server command on your PC in this folder: npx serve . or python -m http.server 8000
  2. Find your PC's IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.15)
  3. Open http://192.168.1.15:8000 in Mobile Chrome or Safari!
Method 2: Host Free Online (GitHub Pages / Vercel)

Upload this index.html to GitHub repository, Netlify, or Vercel for free. You will get a permanent public link (e.g. https://yourname.github.io/wa-logger) accessible anywhere anytime!

Method 3: Direct File Share / WhatsApp

Simply send the file index.html to your phone via WhatsApp or Email. Tap it to open directly inside your phone browser without installing anything!

📱 Add to Home Screen (PWA App):

Once opened on your Android/iPhone browser, tap "Add to Home Screen" in browser menu. It creates a dedicated app icon that works 100% offline!
